Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Screen Translator ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information, specifically regarding the use of the Screen Capture (MediaProjection) API and the floating window overlay (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW).

1. Information We Collect and Process

The core functionality of AI Screen Translator is to provide real-time translation of text appearing on your device's screen. To achieve this, the App requires:

  • Screen Capture (MediaProjection API): With your explicit consent, we capture the selected area of your screen within the translation lens.
  • Display Over Other Apps (Overlay): We use this to float the translation lens and results directly on top of your current app.

2. How We Use the Data

The screen data captured by the App is strictly used for the sole purpose of Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and Translation.

  • Local Processing: Core OCR is performed locally on your device via Google ML Kit.
  • Cloud Processing: Extracted text may be sent securely to the Google Gemini API strictly to generate high-quality translations.

Crucial Note on Screen Recording: We do NOT record video, take permanent screenshots, store your screen capture data locally or remotely, or share your screen contents with any third-party advertisers or analytics services. The image frames are processed in system memory momentarily and are immediately discarded after the text is extracted.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes the following third-party APIs which govern the text data processed:

  • Google APIs (ML Kit & Gemini AI): Data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. User Consent and Control

The App will never capture your screen without your knowledge. You must give explicit system-level permission every time the translation service starts. Furthermore, a persistent notification is displayed while the service is active, providing a clear and immediate "Stop Service" button to terminate all screen access instantly.
