This Privacy Policy explains how the Plushie Blocks mobile app (also shown as "Block Puzzle"; "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The app is a block puzzle game with classic, adventure, daily challenge, jigsaw and claw machine modes and a collection of plush buddies. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

01

Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • your scores and progress: best score, games played, adventure levels completed and the days you finished the daily challenge,
  • unfinished games, so you can continue where you left off,
  • your plush collection and the buddy you picked, and your stock of boosters, revive tickets, claw grabs and lucky wheel spins,
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, language and theme), and which tips you have already seen,
  • the photo you chose to show on the board, if any (see section 2),
  • whether Premium is active, which the app re-checks with Google Play each time it starts.

The whole game runs on your phone; there are no servers of ours. You can delete this data at any time in your device's Settings → Apps → Plushie Blocks → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

02

Your board photo

In Settings → Photo on the board you can choose one of your photos to appear across the blocks you place. The photo is chosen with Android's system photo picker, so the app only receives the single photo you pick and never gets access to the rest of your gallery. A copy is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded or sent anywhere. Remove photo deletes the copy.

03

Google Play Games (optional)

The app can sign in to Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and cloud save. You can decline, and the game works fully without it. When you are signed in:

  • Google shares with the app your Play Games player ID and gamer name (and avatar), so the app can show that you are signed in. The app does not receive your email address.
  • The app submits your best Classic score, the number of adventure levels completed and your daily challenge streak to the game's leaderboards. Other players can see your gamer name and these scores, depending on your Play Games profile settings.
  • Cloud save: a copy of your progress (scores, adventure levels, daily challenge days, plush collection and buddy, boosters, revive tickets and claw grabs) is saved to your Google account through Play Games, so it can be restored on another device. It is stored by Google, not by us. Settings, unfinished games and your board photo are not included.

This information is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can change your Play Games profile visibility, or delete your Play Games data for this game, in the Google Play Games settings on your device or at your Google Account.

04

Device backup

If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the app's progress, settings and board photo in your device backup to your Google account. This backup is made and stored by Google under your account and is governed by Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it. Your Premium status is always confirmed with Google Play instead.

05

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads: a banner on the home screen, a full-screen ad between games now and then, and optional rewarded ads (for example to continue a game, get an extra claw grab or lucky wheel spin, or get a booster). To serve, personalize (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, and on newer Android versions, the Android Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs (Topics and attribution),
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language and app version),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks) and diagnostic data.

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices
In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Settings → Ad privacy in the app.
On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path may vary by device).
Rewarded ads are always optional: the app tells you before one starts. Premium removes all ads from the app.
06

Purchases (Google Play)

The app offers Premium as a subscription (monthly or yearly) and as a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are made through Google Play Billing:

  • Google Play processes the payment. We never receive your card number, billing address or other payment details.
  • The app receives from Google Play only which Premium products your Google account owns and the status of the purchase, so it can unlock Premium on every device signed in with that account.
  • You can cancel or change a subscription at any time in the Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. Restore purchases in the app's settings asks Google Play again for purchases already made.

Payment data is handled under the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

07

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, to talk to Google Play for purchases and to Google Play Games for leaderboards and cloud save.
  • Advertising ID and ad services: used by Google AdMob as described in section 5.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and restore Premium, as described in section 6.
  • Background work (wake lock, foreground service): added by Google's libraries to finish short tasks, such as sending ad measurement data; the app itself runs nothing in the background.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files, and it does not need permission to read your photo library (see section 2).

08

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share the data stored on your device with anyone. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google: for advertising (section 5), for purchases through Google Play (section 6) and, if you sign in, for Google Play Games (section 3).

09

Data retention and security

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google, including Play Games scores and cloud saves, is retained according to Google's policies and your Google account settings. The app is designed to keep your information on your device, but no method of storage is completely secure.

10

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us.

11

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, you can access or delete your data directly on your device at any time. For data processed by Google for Play Games, advertising or payments, you can use the choices in sections 3, 5 and 6 or contact Google. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law; you can contact us with any request.

12

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

13
