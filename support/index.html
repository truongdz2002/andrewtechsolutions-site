Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. None of our apps require sign-up or login, and we do not run servers that store your data.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored on your device. Use the app's Settings to clear history, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

How do I restore a purchase (Chess Master Premium)?

Reinstall the app and open the Premium screen. Purchases are tied to your Google Play or Apple ID account and restore automatically. If not, contact us with your order number.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us. On Android open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. On iOS open Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

How do I control ads?

Ads are served by Google AdMob. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Google or Apple privacy settings.
